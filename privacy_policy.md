# Privacy Policy for TranscodeHQeHQ

**Effective Date:** Dec 1, 2026
**App Name:** TranscodeHQeHQ  
**Developer:** TranscodeHQeHQ Team  

---

## 1. Overview
TranscodeHQeHQ ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is handled when you use the TranscodeHQeHQ mobile application (the "App").

## 2. Data Collection and Processing
**TranscodeHQeHQ is designed with privacy-first principles:**
- **100% On-Device Processing:** All image conversions, video transcodes, and file compression operations are performed locally on your device using native processing engines and local CPU/GPU acceleration.
- **No Uploads:** Your photos, videos, and converted files are **never** uploaded, transmitted, or shared with any external servers, cloud storage, or third parties.

## 3. Storage and Local Data
- **Conversion History & Preferences:** The App stores your recent conversion history records (file names, sizes, timestamps, and output paths) and app preferences (such as theme mode and default output format) locally on your device using encrypted/local storage databases (Hive and SharedPreferences).
- **Gallery Access:** When you save converted media to your device, the App saves them to your public device storage (`Pictures/TranscodeHQeHQ`) so they appear in your gallery. We only access media files that you explicitly select using the system file/image/video picker.

## 4. Permissions
The App requests the following permissions solely for core functionality:
- **Storage / Media Access:** To select media files for conversion and save output files to your device storage.
- **Camera (Optional):** If applicable, for capturing photos to convert directly within the App.

## 5. Third-Party Services
- **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob):** The App may display banner or interstitial advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. You can review Google's privacy policy at [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 6. Children's Privacy
Our App does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the App processes all media locally on-device, no personal data of children is harvested or stored on external servers.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
